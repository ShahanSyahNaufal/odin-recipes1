Ini gambar makanan

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain any excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce to the skillet and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a 9x13 inch baking dish, spread a thin layer of the meat sauce on the bottom.
  6. Layer the cooked lasagna noodles on top of the meat sauce.
  7. Spread a portion of the ricotta cheese mixture over the noodles.
  8. Repeat the layers, ending with a layer of mozzarella cheese.
  9. Bake in the preheated oven for 30-35 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
